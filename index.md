## Biography

Ben Ruby is an independent researcher and author based in **Columbus, Ohio**. His research operates at the intersection of **analytic epistemology, philosophy of mind, and cognitive modeling**. He utilizes foundational frameworks like active inference and Karl Friston's Free Energy Principle (FEP) to map out the explicit thermodynamic and metabolic costs of mental states. 

Outside of his work in cognitive philosophy and theoretical neuroscientific modeling, Ben is an active member of the local Columbus community and owns and operates **Ruby Home and Hauling Services**, balancing the abstract minimization of cognitive entropy with the literal clearance of physical waste and structural clutter.

## Research & Publications

### A Hierarchical Thermodynamic Taxonomy of Doxastic States: An Active Inference Model, Phenomenological Scaling Architecture, and Calibrated Experimental Paradigm
* **Status:** Preprint configured for Zenodo / PhilPapers Repository Submission
* **Abstract:** Traditional analytic epistemology treats belief, disbelief, and suspended judgment as idealized, cost-free propositional attitudes. However, the biological brain is an asset-heavy, metabolically constrained organ operating under strict thermodynamic boundaries. Integrating Karl Friston’s Free Energy Principle (FEP) with empirical neuroimaging constraints, we present a materialist architecture of cognition: the Thermodynamic State-Space Model (TSSM). We demonstrate that doxastic states do not share a uniform metabolic baseline. Instead, passive belief occupies automated subcortical pathways of low resistance within the ventromedial prefrontal cortex (vmPFC) and basal ganglia networks. Active disbelief operates via a dual-route trajectory managed either via a low-overhead subcortical mismatch loop routed through the anterior insula and anterior cingulate cortex (ACC) to execute categorical rejections of obvious contradictions, or via frontoparietal pathways (rIFG/vlPFC) during resource-heavy active counter-arguing. Conversely, the true suspension of judgment represents a highly active, resource-intensive prefrontal equilibrium managed by the dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (dlPFC) and superior parietal gating routines. To resolve critical limitations regarding informational-biophysical translation, fMRS voxel overlapping, and baseline shifts in worldview disruption, we introduce non-linear structural paradigm modulations alongside the Forced-Choice Asymmetric Betting Paradigm (FCABP)—an fMRI-compatible behavioral model utilizing an adaptive staircasing design, multi-modal physiological nuisance controls, and quantitative metabolic calibration to isolate neurocomputational synaptic workloads under asymmetric risk and environmental ambiguity barriers.
* **Links:** https://philpapers.org/rec/RUBATT-4

### An Active Inference Model of Cognitive States and Epistemic Parity
* **Status:** Research Framework Entry
* **Abstract:** This computational model details the transactional mechanisms of an active inference agent undergoing policy selection across varying environmental ambiguity conditions. By parameterizing somatic energy reserves as a top-down precision multiplier over epistemic feedback channels, the framework mathematically tracks how resource depletion drives an adaptive collapse into low-entropy subcortical default networks, establishing formal biophysical constraints for traditional philosophical assertions of epistemic parity.
