## Biography
Ben Ruby is an independent researcher and author based in **Columbus, Ohio**. His research operates at the intersection of **analytic epistemology, philosophy of mind, and cognitive modeling**. He utilizes frameworks like active inference and the Free Energy Principle (FEP) to map out the thermodynamic and metabolic costs of mental states. 

Outside of his cognitive philosophy and theoretical writing, Ben is an active member of the Columbus community and operates **Ruby Home and Hauling Services**.
